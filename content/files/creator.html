Basics

Artwork guide and templates – sizes, what the card covers, and how the pop-out mask works.

Place the artworksaved as 1024 × 1024, the size the game uses

Drag the picture to move it; the mouse wheel zooms.

Action

Something the player chooses to do during their turn, instead of something that happens by itself. Pick its kind here – Duty, Ferocity, Pray, Agenda or Oath, or a plain action – and the card gets that keyword automatically. A card has at most one action.

Keywords

Some keywords work by themselves (Flying, Stealth, Vanguard): just add them. Others, like Rally, Artifice or Strike, say when something happens: pick one and a box opens under it for what happens. Actions (Duty, Ferocity, Pray, Agenda, Oath) are set up in the Action section above. Ephemeral works by itself (the card leaves your hand at the end of the turn) and is only found on stratagems. The number after each name is how many official cards use it.

Abilities

For abilities that belong to no keyword. An ability is one trigger followed by one or more effects, resolved in order. Entries used by many official cards are the safest choices; ones marked 0 exist in the engine but no official card uses them.

    Game account
    
      
    
      
    
        
    A game login file lets the game sign in as this game account without Steam – for another computer, or when Steam is not working – so you keep your decks and progress. Put the file in the game's folder under UserData\WarpforgeRevival\ and start the game. While the file is there the game always signs in with it.
    
        
    You normally do not need it: signing in from the game's own sign-in window writes this file for you. Treat it like a password: anyone who has the file can play as you. Changing your password or a ban cancels every file and game sign-in at once; signing out in the game cancels that computer's or phone's.

    Bug reports
    
      
    
        How to report a bug (testers): in the game press F8 (PC) or Report bug in the Testers window right when it happens: the screenshot and log are taken at that moment.
        One bug per report; the title says what went wrong ("Bolter Brother dealt 0 damage to a warlord"), not the cause.
        Check this list first: if it is already here, still send a short report and put the other report's number in the title ("same as B-0042") — your device and version are what we need.
        If the game crashes or freezes, just start it again: it offers to send a report with everything saved at the time.

    Word filter
    
      
    Words blocked in chat (starred out) and in player and account names (refused). One per line; capitals do not matter, and look-alike spellings (sh1t, f.u.c.k, fuuuck) are caught. word blocks the whole word only, word* any word starting with it, *word* it anywhere, even inside other words (careful: that catches innocent words too). Names chosen before a word was added are not changed.

    The short version
    
    
      
      
    • Every card's art is one 1024 × 1024 picture. Bring any size or shape: the card creator's editor crops and scales it for you.
      • 
      
    • Troops, vehicles and warlords use a tall painting, background included, in the middle of that square. It does not need a transparent background.
      • 
      
    • Stratagems use the whole square.
      • 
      
    • Parts that break out over the card frame (a gun barrel, a banner) are optional and come from a second picture, the pop-out mask.
      •

    GuideWhat it means
    
          
    Gold boxThe picture area: 670 pixels wide (x 177 to 847), the full 1024 high. Fill all of it. Nothing outside it is used.
    
          
    Dashed lineThe edge of the card. The frame covers a thin band just inside it.
    
          
    Clear zoneShown at full brightness. Put the face or focal point here.
    
          
    Red lineThe card's name and rules text can start about here. The picture carries on behind the text but is darkened. Short text starts lower, so the picture should still look good down there.
    
          
    Red circlesCovered by the faction badge, cost, attack values, armour and health.
    
          
    Gold tintOutside the card. Only seen where the pop-out mask says so.
    
        
    
      
    
    
    

    
    The pop-out mask (optional)
    
    
    Official cards let part of the figure overlap the frame. That is controlled by a second picture of the same size: white where the art should be drawn on top of the frame, black everywhere else. Soft grey edges give a soft blend.
    
    
      
      
    • Start from art-mask-blank.png and paint white over the part that should break out. Keep it small: a weapon, a hand, a crest. The official cards pop out one or two details, not the whole figure.
      • 
      
    • The mask must line up with the picture as saved by the creator. The safe way: place and save the artwork in the creator first, then paint the mask over that saved 1024 × 1024 picture.
      • 
      
    • No mask means everything stays neatly inside the frame. That is fine, and it is the default.
      •

    Picture quality
    
    
      
      
    • PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 20 MB. The creator saves it as a 1024 × 1024 PNG.
      • 
      
    • For units aim for at least 670 × 1024; for stratagems at least 1024 × 1024. Smaller pictures are enlarged and look soft.
      • 
      
    • Unit pictures are portrait (about 2 : 3). A landscape picture will be cropped hard at the sides.
      • 
      
    • Only upload artwork you made or have the right to use, and follow the card creator rules.
      •
